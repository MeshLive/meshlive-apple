MeshLive lets you call people near you, and watch live video from nearby phones, directly over Bluetooth. There is no internet, SIM card or account to set up.

CALL PEOPLE NEARBY
- Voice and video calls between phones that are close to each other, end-to-end encrypted.
- Hold to talk when you only want your microphone on for a moment.
- Add people you trust by scanning each other's code. Nothing goes through a server.

WATCH AND LISTEN
- Watch livestreams from nearby phones.
- Listen to audio-only livestreams, like a local radio, with the screen off.

YOU DECIDE WHAT PEOPLE SEE
- Nearby phones normally see a generated name and a temporary ID that changes every 15 minutes.
- Your real name goes only to the people you allow.
- Block or report anyone from a call, a livestream or the list of people nearby.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. MeshLive collects no data.
- Calls and streams are encrypted. A nearby Bluetooth scanner can still tell that a MeshLive device is there, and MeshLive does not make you anonymous.
- Delete everything erases your keys, contacts and settings from the phone.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Both people need MeshLive and must be within about 20 metres of each other.
- iOS lets MeshLive be found only while the app is open. Keep it open to receive calls: a closed app cannot ring.
- Works with MeshLive on other iPhones and on Android phones.
- You must be 18 or older. MeshLive does not replace calling emergency services.
