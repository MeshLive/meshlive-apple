MeshLive connects nearby iPhones, Macs and Android phones directly over Bluetooth for voice and video calls and for
watching livestreams. There is no account, no sign-in, no server and no internet use, so there is no demo account.

HOW TO TRY IT
Full function needs a second device running MeshLive within about 20 metres. A screen recording of two devices in a call
and watching a livestream is linked in the "Notes" video field: <the owner adds the link in App Store Connect>.
1. First launch: the Terms page asks for "Agree and continue", then one "Continue" shows the Bluetooth, microphone and
   notification prompts one after another. The camera is asked for the first time a video call starts.
2. Open MeshLive on both devices. On one, open Calls, then tap the other device's generated name (for example "artsy cascade").
3. On the other device tap Accept. Every call must be accepted by the person called; nothing is received without it.
4. Livestreams from nearby devices appear on the Live tab; tap one to watch it. This version watches and listens; it does
   not broadcast.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (guideline 1.2)
- Terms with "zero tolerance for abuse" are shown at first launch and must be accepted ("Agree and continue"). The app is
  rated 18+.
- Block and Report are offered on an incoming call from a caller who showed a real key (Decline and block, Report), on a
  livestream whose source authenticated itself (Block this person, Report), on Recent rows, on a contact and on the list of
  nearby people. Block hides the person on the device. Report also blocks them and opens a prepared email to the team
  (meshlive.dev@gmail.com) that the user reviews and sends. Settings > About > Contact us is a second route. A livestream
  whose source did not authenticate offers Hide this livestream instead.
- MeshLive has no servers, so the team cannot see or remove content; blocking and hiding on the device is how a user stops
  contact, and reports are read by the team and used to improve these controls.

BACKGROUND MODES
- audio: keeps the sound of a call that is in progress going when the screen locks or the user switches app.
- bluetooth-central and bluetooth-peripheral: keep the Bluetooth link of a call or of a stream being watched alive in the
  background. When the app is in the background and no call or stream is active, it stops all Bluetooth scanning and
  advertising. The app does not use CallKit or push notifications.

ENCRYPTION
The app uses only Apple's CryptoKit for cryptography (key agreement, signatures and authenticated encryption of calls and
streams). Opus and libvpx are audio and video codecs and do no encryption. ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is set to NO.

PERMISSIONS
Bluetooth (find and connect to nearby devices), microphone (calls), camera (video calls and scanning a contact's code),
notifications (so the device rings for a call). No location, contacts, photos or tracking.

PRIVACY
The app collects no data. It has no analytics or advertising. Everything stays on the device except what the user sends to
the other person in a call or stream. The privacy policy is at https://meshlive.github.io/privacy/.

OPEN SOURCE
MeshLive is open source (GNU GPL v3 or later); the licences of Opus and libvpx are shown in Settings > About > Open-source
licences. The mention of Android is a factual statement that the app interoperates with MeshLive for Android.
